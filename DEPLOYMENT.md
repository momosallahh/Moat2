## 🌐 Deploy to the Internet (Choose One)

### Option 1: Netlify Drop (FASTEST - 30 seconds)

1. Go to **https://app.netlify.com/drop**
2. Drag and drop the **entire Moat2 folder**
3. Done! You'll get a live URL like `https://random-name.netlify.app`

**Want a custom URL?**
- After deployment, go to Site Settings → Domain Management
- Change to something like `t-fa-fashion.netlify.app`

---

### Option 2: Vercel (Also Very Fast)

1. Go to **https://vercel.com/new**
2. Click "Add New" → "Project"
3. Upload the folder or connect your GitHub
4. Click "Deploy"
5. Done! Live in ~1 minute

---

### Option 3: GitHub Pages (Free Forever)

1. Go to your GitHub repository
2. Click **Settings** → **Pages**
3. Under "Source", select your branch: `claude/tfa-luxury-fashion-ecommerce-01ELRhpDC5USmBNJysJx9ENj`
4. Click **Save**
5. Your site will be live at: `https://momosallahh.github.io/Moat2/`
