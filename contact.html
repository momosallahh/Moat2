Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within the US takes 3-5 business days. International shipping varies by location but typically takes 7-14 business days. All pieces are carefully packaged to ensure they arrive in perfect condition.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 14-day return policy for unworn, unaltered items in their original packaging. Custom-designed pieces are final sale. Please contact us before initiating a return.

How do I care for my T-Fa pieces?

Each piece comes with specific care instructions. Generally, we recommend spot cleaning or dry cleaning for our luxury pieces. The Hat should be stored in its dust bag. The Bodysuit can be hand washed cold. The Two-in-One is dry clean only.

How long does custom design take?

Custom designs typically take 8-12 weeks from consultation to completion. Rush orders may be available for an additional fee. Timeline depends on design complexity and material availability.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! All T-Fa pieces can be gift wrapped at no additional charge. Simply select the gift wrapping option at checkout and include a personal message.

Can I schedule a private appointment?

Absolutely. We welcome private appointments at our atelier. Contact us to schedule a time to experience the collection in person, receive personal styling, or discuss custom designs.
